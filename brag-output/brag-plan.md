# Brag Plan: Need for Madness — Remastered

## What is this app?
A browser remake of the 2005 Java racing game *Need for Madness* — rebuilt from the original game's own code and models, with Career, Free Play, 52 cars and online races.

## The angle
A love letter to a game people played in a school computer lab. The video treats a 20-year-old Java .jar as a prestige sports-car launch: real in-game footage, cinematic type, orange accent. The joke/charm is the contrast — low-poly madness presented with total seriousness.

## Hook (0–2.6s)
Black. A line types: "Remember Need for Madness?" Held long enough to read, then a hard cut on the beat.

## Key moments
- Main menu lockup: NEED FOR MADNESS / REMASTERED with the car turntable behind it.
- Car select: "RADICAL ONE", stat bars (Top speed 9, Acceleration 7, Stunts 10) fill one by one; counter reads 52 cars.
- Stage select: aerial view of "THE INTRODUCTORY STAGE" · 4 LAPS.
- Race: the monster truck on the grid, then 285 KM/H, 7 cars.

## Outro / punchline
"Rebuilt. Still mad." over the logo, with CAREER · FREE PLAY · ONLINE.

## User flow worth showing
Menu → pick a car → pick a stage → race. All captured from the real running game (headless Chromium, 1280×720).

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: prestige car-launch trailer for a game from a school computer lab
- Interpretation: big condensed italic type, hard cuts on strong beats, short holds, restraint with SFX.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 21s

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support, rhythmic bed
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12 (≈110 BPM)
- Music treatment: starts at 0s, low under the hook, full from the reveal, fade out over the last 1.5s
- Music cue guidance: preset read. Strong cues 8.74s, 13.11s, 17.47s, 18.56s; beat grid ≈0.545s.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none (restraint; footage is already busy)
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched (key ticks on the hook, ticks on stat bars, one impact on the title)
- Restraint rule: SFX never cover the music's strong beats.
